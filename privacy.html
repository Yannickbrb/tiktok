Privacy Policy — Media Agency

Last updated: May 6, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Media Agency collects, uses, and handles information when you use the Media Agency application and its integrations with third-party platforms including TikTok.

1. About Media Agency

Media Agency is a private automated content publishing application owned and operated by a single individual. Media Agency is not a public application and is used exclusively by its owner to automate social media content publishing.

2. Information Media Agency Accesses

Media Agency accesses the following information through the TikTok API solely to enable its core publishing functionality:

PermissionPurpose
user.info.basicRetrieve display name and profile information to confirm the correct TikTok account is connected to Media Agency.
video.uploadUpload short-form video files to the authenticated TikTok account on behalf of the owner.
video.publishPublish uploaded videos as public posts on the authenticated TikTok account.

3. How Media Agency Uses Information

Media Agency uses the accessed information solely to publish video content on behalf of the authenticated account owner. Media Agency does not use this information for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond its core publishing function.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Media Agency does not store any TikTok user data on external or third-party servers. All data processed by Media Agency, including API tokens and credentials, remains exclusively on the owner's local machine or private server. Media Agency does not transmit user data to any external service other than the official TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram APIs.

5. Data Sharing

Media Agency does not sell, trade, rent, transfer, or share any user data with any third parties under any circumstances whatsoever.

6. Third-Party Platform Integrations

Media Agency integrates with the following third-party platforms. Each platform's own privacy policy applies to data processed by their respective APIs:

PlatformPrivacy Policy
TikToktiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
YouTube (Google)policies.google.com/privacy
Instagram (Meta)privacycenter.instagram.com/policy

7. Security

All API credentials and access tokens used by Media Agency are stored locally on the owner's machine and are never transmitted to any external service other than the respective platform APIs for authentication purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

Media Agency is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. Media Agency does not knowingly collect or process information from children under 13 years of age.

9. Your Rights

As Media Agency does not collect or store personal data from users other than the application owner, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. If you have concerns about data processed by TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram, please refer to their respective privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in Media Agency's functionality or applicable legal requirements. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.

11. Contact

For any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the data practices of Media Agency, please contact the application owner directly.
